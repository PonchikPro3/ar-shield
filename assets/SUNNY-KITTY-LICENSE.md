# Sunny Kitty

Author: **Ponter** — https://sketchfab.com/ponter

Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sunny-kitty-bdd7369e67f74ff89dc74273879db395

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The original `sunny_kitty.glb` is preserved. For Unity, its static mesh is
converted to OBJ and its embedded base-color texture is extracted to PNG.
No mesh simplification is applied. The scene centers and scales the model.

Attribution is taken from the GLB's embedded asset metadata.
